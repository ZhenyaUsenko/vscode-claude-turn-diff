# Closed runs on Opus 4.6 at high effort

Opus 4.6 caught 11 of the 13 bugs, with no false alarm on clean code. Leaving out the two crash bugs, it flagged 15 of the 25 real failures, against 18 for both Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 at the same effort.

It was also the slowest: 4.8 minutes per run against 1.6 for Opus 5.5. At about the same cost, Opus 5.5 at extra-high effort flags 22.

## How it ran

It ran exactly like the other closed batches, with `--model claude-opus-4-6 --effort high`:

- headless CLI sessions;
- the closed instructions as the whole system prompt;
- only `Bash` and `Read`;
- the 5-minute cache and the crash rule;
- the same 15 conditions.

All 15 ran at once instead of 8 at a time, to use what was left of a five-hour window that was about to reset.

The Opus 5.5 high batch ran before the crash rule, so its crash rows are counted differently.

- **index-mtime, missed**: it passed `capture.same_size_edit_a_second_later`. It did not see that the index copy gets a fresh modification time, so git skips a same-size edit made within the same second. Every Opus 5.5 and Fable batch caught this.
- **binary-default, 6 of 8**: besides the statement-bound miss every batch shares, it passed `view.a_m_d_become_the_right_sides`. With the bug, added and deleted files count as binary and are skipped, so the diff never shows them at all.
- **interrupt-equality, 1 of 2**: it caught the turn not being finished, but passed `running.interrupted_turn_stops_growing`, which follows from the same defect.
- **pure-rename-dropped, missed**, like Opus 5.5 at high effort.
- **stamp-seconds, 1 of 3**, the same as every high-effort batch.
- **No extras**, not even the legitimate outside-files ordering failure that Opus 5.5 raised.

## Speed, tokens and cost

| | Opus 4.6, high | Fable 5.1, high | Opus 5.5, high | Opus 5.5, extra high |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Real failures flagged, 11 non-crash bugs | 15 of 25 | 18 of 25 | 18 of 25 | 22 of 25 |
| Time per run, median | 4.8 min | 2.3 min | 1.6 min | 3.2 min |
| Output tokens per run, median | 14k | 12k | 11k | 22k |
| Output tokens per second, median | 49 | 86 | 115 | 117 |
| Uncached input per run, median | 26k | 29k | 27k | 27k |
| Final context per run, median | 39k | 41k | 40k | 54k |
| Cost on the 5-minute cache, 15 runs | $8.27 | $13.94 | $5.37 | $8.58 |

- **Price**: Opus 5.5 rates would give $6.62 for the same tokens, so Opus 4.6 costs 1.25 times as much per token.
- **Speed**: it wrote a similar amount at less than half the speed, so runs took three times as long. It ran 15 at once rather than 8, but Opus 5.5 showed no slowdown up to 20 at once in the first experiment.
- **Progress from 4.6 to 5.5**, closed at high effort: 3 more real failures, three times the speed, and 35% lower cost. Opus 5.5 at extra high costs about the same as Opus 4.6 at high and flags 7 more.
- **Five-hour window**: the batch straddled a reset, so there is no clean figure for it.

## Also fixed

`claude/run-cli.mjs` stopped launching new runs when a run reported no five-hour reading, which happened right after the reset. It now stops only on a reading at or above the threshold. All 15 runs had already launched, so this batch was unaffected.

## Caveats

- **One run per bug.** Single tests vary between runs.
